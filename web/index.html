AirScribe Gesture & Command Guide

Full touchless hand tracking controls & keyboard shortcuts

🖐️ Real-Time Hand Gestures

☝️ 1 Finger Up

Draw / Freehand

Raise your index finger only. Write natural strokes, highlighter marks, or drag to form geometric shapes.

✌️ 2 Fingers Up

Hover & Navigate

Peace sign (Index + Middle UP). Move your virtual pointer freely across the canvas without leaving ink.

🤏 Pinch Tips

Instant Click

Touch your Thumb tip and Index fingertip together to immediately click any hovered toolbar button or color swatch.

🖐️ Open Palm / 3+

Quick Eraser

Open your palm or raise 3+ fingers. Instantly transforms into a wide eraser ring to wipe away mistakes.

⏳ 0.5s Hover Dwell

Dwell-to-Select

Hold your 2-finger cursor over any toolbar button for half a second to automatically click it without touching your screen.

✊ Closed Fist

Pause / Standby

Curl all fingers into a gentle fist to commit your active shape and pause drawing without triggering accidental marks.
